Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

Lunar ("the App") is developed by Ordinary Code ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

  • Anonymous User ID — A randomly generated UUID created on first launch and stored on your device. This is used solely to sync your portfolio across reinstalls. It is never linked to your name, email address, or any other personal identifier.
  • Portfolio Holdings — The coins, quantities, purchase prices, and dates you enter in the Portfolio tab. This data is stored on our servers to enable sync.
  • App Usage — Anonymised usage data may be collected by Google AdMob and Firebase Analytics to serve relevant ads and improve the app.

How We Use Your Information

  • To store and retrieve your portfolio data.
  • To display personalised advertisements to free users via Google AdMob.
  • We do not sell your data to any third parties.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or financial account details.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — Serves ads to free users. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • CoinGecko — Provides market price data. No personal data is shared.
  • Amazon Web Services — Hosts our backend infrastructure in Sydney, Australia (ap-southeast-2).
  • Anthropic (via AWS Bedrock) — Powers AI market analysis. No personal data is included in analysis requests.

Data Retention

Your portfolio data is retained for as long as you use the app. You can delete all your data at any time by contacting us at theordinarycode@gmail.com with your User ID (found in Settings).

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
