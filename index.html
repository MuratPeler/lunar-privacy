Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

Lunar ("the App") is developed by Ordinary Code ("we", "us"). We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. This policy explains how the app works and what third-party services are used.

🔒 Everything you enter into Lunar stays on your device. We never see it.

Your Data Stays on Your Device

Everything you enter into Lunar — your portfolio holdings, purchase prices, and dates — is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. It is never sent to us or any server we operate. We have no access to it and no ability to recover it if you uninstall the app.

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or any personal identifier.
  • We do not collect your portfolio or financial data.
  • We do not operate any accounts or require any sign-up.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.

Third-Party Services

The following third-party services are used by the app. Each operates under its own privacy policy.

  • Google AdMob — Used to display advertisements to free users. AdMob may collect anonymised device and usage data to serve relevant ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • CoinGecko — Provides cryptocurrency market prices. Only your requested coin symbols are sent; no personal data is shared.
  • Amazon Web Services (Sydney, ap-southeast-2) — Hosts our backend for market data, AI signals, and news aggregation. No personal data is transmitted to or stored on these servers.
  • Anthropic (via AWS Bedrock) — Powers the AI market analysis feature. Requests contain only public price and volume data — no personal information.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
